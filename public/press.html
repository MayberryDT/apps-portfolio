Questions

Common questions.

Who is Tyler Mayberry?

Tyler Mayberry is an AI systems builder and practical product engineer. He builds agentic workflows, internal tools, automation systems and AI-native software for messy real-world work. His main project is ibara (ibara.app), which gives AI agents computers of their own. His company, Cirlet (cirlet.com), helps independent insurance agencies put AI and automation to work. He goes by MayberryDT on GitHub.

What is ibara?

ibara is Tyler Mayberry’s project that gives AI agents computers of their own. The agents use the browser and desktop apps there while the owner keeps working on their own computer. Any agent that speaks MCP can drive it. It is built for Omarchy. No other platform is supported or planned. Its site is ibara.app; it is unrelated to ibara.ai.

What is Cirlet?

Cirlet is Tyler Mayberry’s company. It helps independent insurance agencies put AI and automation to work. Its site is cirlet.com.

What has Tyler Mayberry built?

His projects include ibara (computers of their own for AI agents), Siege of Champions (a 3v3 MOBA in the browser), ytfast (a native YouTube Music player for Omarchy), Rat Detective Online (a multiplayer browser game), Chartroom (a local second brain built on GBrain), ChartStead (conference programming and speaker management), Masthead (a local record of coding-agent sessions) and Pip (a spending companion built around Spendable Cash Today). Those, plus Hotel Cleaning Schedule, Executioner, Milkbench, Self-Hosted AI Agent, Helm, his Omarchy themes and tools, and the Prepare Project and Record Everything agent skills, are on the laptop in his studio at tylermayberry.dev. His company is Cirlet.

Main project

ibara

Game

Siege of Champions

Siege of Champions is a 3v3 MOBA that runs in a desktop browser: two lanes, a forest jungle and six fantasy champions. Play solo with bots or in a private room with friends; bots fill empty slots. An early playtest, updated in numbered patches.

Game site

Open-source desktop app

ytfast

ytfast is a native YouTube Music player for Omarchy, written in Rust with egui. Audio only, themed by Omarchy, with no browser engine, no telemetry and no server of its own. Unofficial and not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

Source code

Product build

ChartStead

ChartStead is conference programming and speaker management software. Keep the call, review, speaker work, messages, and agenda together, then publish the public program when your team is ready. A live site, sample-data demo, and MIT-licensed source.

Product site

Product build

Masthead

Masthead is a local-first session data layer for AI coding agents with SQLite-backed capture, Workbench publishing, Logbook artifacts, and read-only MCP retrieval. Electron app for macOS and Linux.

Product site

AI-native product build

Pip

Pip is an AI-native spending companion built around Spendable Cash Today, deterministic application logic, read-only account data, and agent-assisted explanation and approved actions.

Product site

Self-Hosted AI Agent

Self-Hosted AI Agent is a plain guide to the computer a self-hosted AI agent runs on: whether the one you have is enough, what it costs to run, and when a Mac mini is worth it.

Usage notes

Use and avoid.

Use "Tyler Mayberry" as the primary person entity. Use "Cirlet" as Tyler’s company. Use "ibara", in lowercase, for his main project. Use "AI systems builder and practical product engineer" as the preferred role line.

  • Do not present Pip as a bank, financial adviser, money movement product, or guarantee.
  • Do not present ChartStead as an all-in-one event suite, automatic scheduler, or production-complete conference platform.
  • Do not describe Cirlet as the primary entity over Tyler Mayberry when writing about Tyler personally.
  • Do not confuse ibara (ibara.app) with ibara.ai, an unrelated company.
  • Other people share the name. This Tyler Mayberry is, goes by MayberryDT and runs Cirlet.
  • When possible, link to this page, the About page, and the relevant product or case-study page.
