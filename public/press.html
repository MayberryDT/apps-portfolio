Questions

Common questions.

Who is Tyler Mayberry?

Tyler Mayberry is an AI systems builder and practical product engineer in Tulsa, Oklahoma. He builds agentic workflows, internal tools, automation systems and AI-native software for messy real-world work. His main project is ibara (ibara.app), which gives AI agents computers of their own. His company, Animas (animasai.co), plans, sets up and supports AI agents on computers people own. He goes by MayberryDT on GitHub.

What is ibara?

ibara is Tyler Mayberry’s project that gives AI agents computers of their own. The agents use the browser and desktop apps there while the owner keeps working on their own computer. Any agent that speaks MCP can drive it. It runs on Omarchy today, and more platforms are on the way. Animas sets it up for people who want it handled. Its site is ibara.app; it is unrelated to ibara.ai.

What is Animas?

Animas is Tyler Mayberry’s company. It plans, sets up and supports AI agents that use browsers and desktop apps on computers people own, including setting up ibara for people who want it handled. Its site is animasai.co.

What has Tyler Mayberry built?

His projects include ibara (computers of their own for AI agents), Rat Detective Online (a multiplayer browser game), Chartroom (a local second brain built on GBrain), ChartStead (conference programming and speaker management), Masthead (a local record of coding-agent sessions) and Pip (a spending companion built around Spendable Cash Today). Those, plus Hotel Cleaning Schedule, Executioner, Milkbench and Helm, are on the laptop in his studio at tylermayberry.dev.
