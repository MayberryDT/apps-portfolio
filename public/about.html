Bio

Builder first.

I build software around the part of work that usually gets skipped: the context, handoffs, judgment boundaries, review loops, and interfaces that make a tool usable after the demo.

My work spans AI-agent workflows, internal dashboards, automation, product prototypes, and AI-native application surfaces. I use Animas AI as the studio and business wrapper for this work, while this site is the canonical personal site for Tyler Mayberry.

The common thread is practical product engineering. I like turning vague, high-friction work into concrete systems people can understand, operate, and improve.
