ibara: a computer you own for your agents

OpenAI, Meta and xAI all just gave their agents a computer.

But it's only available in their cloud, for a VM that's most likely less powerful than the potato in your closet.

ibara for Omarchy gives your agent a PC you own.

Free and Open Source

ibara gives your agents computer use across the Omarchy machines you own.

The agent gets a spare's desktop, browser and apps, and you watch, take control and hand back.

Works with any agent. Get connected with a copy/paste prompt.

Checked, not just claimed.

Your agent checks the work it made as a real user on a real machine.

ibara tracks every task leaving a complete log of everything the agent does for review.

Your hardware. Your connection.

Keep all your logins and access, or restrict it in any way that you want.

Computer use with a real home ip and no expensive cloud bill.

Basic hardware is plenty, ibara idles at 17.8 MiB.

You choose who's driving.

Watch the spare from the console, take control when you want, hand it back when you're done.

One driver at a time. By default ibara asks before an agent sends, spends or deletes, and always asks before changing access.

(you can also 'dangerously' allow complete access)

132 of 157 runs passed the benchmark's checks.

Claude Code with Opus 5.5 and Codex with GPT-6-Astra each passed 22 of 23.

Even @opencode on the free plan passed 14 of 22 using big-pickle.

Big thanks to @trycua here.

Grok Bot, Dots and Muse give an agent its own computer in the vendor's cloud.

OpenClaw and Hermes run on your hardware but are the agent.

ibara is a separate computer you own that any agent can use.

Two Omarchy 4 computers, Tailscale on both, and one agent (even free @opencode works).

Install on each, add the spare from the console, copy the connect prompt to your agent and give it a first task.

I made it as plug-and-play as possible.

Start with one spare. Connect the rest.

Lightweight, built for basic hardware, free and open source.

Install ibara, or take over the demo in your browser first.
