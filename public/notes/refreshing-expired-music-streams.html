Refreshing expired music streams

By Tyler Mayberry · Author link added 24 October 2026

Shipped Fast YouTube Music 0.2.0.

Pick a remix chip and your next song becomes that mix.

I took the little mix chips straight from YouTube Music.

Now when you click 'Pump Up' or 'Workout' it will recreate the playlist for you based on that genre.

Also made the giant art clickable to play/pause.

Now when you click a song it does this cool little animation I wanted.

Click any song in Music now and its cover flies straight into the big Now Playing view.

I also fixed a stall bug, which was the real reason for this new release. I just added the rest as the cherry on top.

After about 6 hours, YouTube's stream links expire.

On Repeat one, mpv kept looping the dead link. 20,000 times a second. Now it grabs a fresh one.

Also fixed songs cutting out early, mostly in long mixes or after a long pause.

Flickering between the buffering spinner and the pause button with no sound: that's what the stall looked like, if you ever hit it.
