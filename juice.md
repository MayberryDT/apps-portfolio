# juice.md — tylermayberry.dev

For creative work, read the compass and only the directions that match the task.
Apply them with make-it-juicy. Update states and discoveries as you go.

## Compass

**Feeling:** stepping into someone's quiet working room and being allowed to
look around. It should feel calm, warm and personal, found rather than
presented. It plays like a modern realistic point-and-click adventure, not a
website.

**Character:** photographic realism (walnut, daylight, real belongings).
Liquid glass that belongs to the scene. Objects that respond physically when
picked up. First-person stories behind the things in the room.

**Protected:** the plan's [protected design](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#protected-design)
and the voice and truth rules in [COPYWRITING.md](COPYWRITING.md). Juice never
overrides them.

## Directions

**Arrival: the doorway, then a slow walk in.** Read when changing the hero,
Enter, room entry or the Outside return. The entrance should feel like
approaching a real place: unhurried, with one clear way in. *Accepted:* Tyler
accepted the slower journey and Outside return (v40, 2026-09-12, after the
400 ms fade-start fix). He chose Enter option 09, a warm charcoal button that
belongs to the room.

**Things answer your hand.** Read for any inspection, phone or shelf prop. A
picked-up object lifts, turns within limits and settles back to rest, so it
feels held rather than swapped. *Accepted:* return-to-rest animation (Tyler,
2026-09-11). The photographic still hands off to the live model.

**Honest surfaces and light.** Read before any new art, reveal or model. Real
contact shadows, cable routing and material finish matter more than polish.
Plastic, over-shiny or rendered-looking results break the spell. *Accepted
principle:* Tyler rejected the artificial v7 desk (2026-09-10) and the shiny,
blurry hero background (2026-09-12). The v8 desk became the accepted standard.

**Every belonging has a story.** Read for photos, shelf items, Notes and project
copy. Each object leads to a specific, lived reason it is there. *Applied:* v49
copy, now live; Tyler accepted it for commit on 2026-09-13.

**Native, exact details.** Read for screens and devices. Reproduce the real
thing instead of a lookalike. *Applied:* the Omarchy monitor uses Tyler's exact
ASCII screensaver with native terminal effects (R25).

**A second look pays off.** Read for idle or ambient moments. A small, quiet
reward for lingering or coming back, such as a subtle light shift or a detail
noticed on return. *Idea only:* it must keep room views photographic, stay
cheap to run and respect reduced motion.
