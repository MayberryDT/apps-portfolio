Builder thesis

The useful layer is the workflow.

Practical AI work is not just a model call. It is the interface, context, constraints, memory, review loop, and operational fit around the model.

Tyler builds tools around real operational friction.

The throughline is practical AI systems, internal tools, automation, workflow design, product prototypes, and software for real operational problems.
