# COPYWRITING — tylermayberry.dev

This is the audience-and-meaning brief for a personal, non-commercial site. It
owns audience, meaning and voice. [juice.md](juice.md) builds the site's felt
character from it. The [plan](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) owns delivery.

## Working brief

- **What it is:** Tyler Mayberry's personal working studio, somewhere to get
  away and focus. Visitors explore the room to find his projects, interests,
  photos, notes and contact.
- **Purpose (Tyler's intention, 2026-09-08 brief):** personal discovery and
  showing the work. The site itself demonstrates the work. It is not a sales
  funnel. The older “job applications and consulting credibility” positioning
  is historical.
- **Who arrives:** this is an inference, not research. Likely visitors are
  people who have heard Tyler's name or seen one of his projects: peers,
  collaborators, possible clients or employers, and friends. They arrive from
  a shared link, a search for his name or a project page. The 2026-09-13 Search
  Console export shaped URL preservation only; it is not an audience study.
- **Desired response:** curiosity, and a feel for who Tyler is and how he works.
  Visitors should want to look around, and reaching him through the Contact
  phone should be easy. Nothing is forced.
- **Left open on purpose:** visitors choose their own path. The room does not
  explain everything.

## Voice

- Tyler speaks in the first person. Start with plain words (GOV.UK clarity),
  then give them his own rhythm. This is the v49 method, which Tyler accepted
  on 2026-09-13.
- Be specific and lived: real objects, places, why a project exists, what
  worked and what didn't. Keep finished and unfinished work distinct. The Notes
  say “stopping is an outcome”.
- Warm and understated. No hype, no résumé tone, no sales language.
- Keep functional labels short. Headings use sentence case.
- Example, the live desk introduction: “When I want to follow an idea and work
  on it, this is the place: my personal working studio, projects and the things
  around them sharing the room.”

## Truth and privacy

- Prototypes show building and judgment. They are not traction; never invent
  users, revenue or endorsements.
- Education reads “6 years studying at Oklahoma State University in Stillwater,
  Oklahoma”, with no degree details.
- Private vision scripts are aspirations, never biography.
- New personal details, photos or private research need Tyler's approval
  before publication.
- Keep the About and Press content that preserves search unless Tyler asks for
  a change.
