# COPYWRITING — tylermayberry.dev

This is the audience-and-meaning brief for Tyler's personal studio, and it owns
audience, meaning and voice. The felt character lives in [juice.md](juice.md),
required behaviour in the [spec](docs/SPEC.md), and delivery in the
[plan](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

## Working brief

- **What it is:** Tyler Mayberry's personal studio, a photographic room you
  explore. It is the home of everything he makes. Cirlet is his company.
- **What it's for:** an experience, not efficient research. In Tyler's words
  (2026-09-28): “I'm not trying to optimize this so that people can research
  me. I'm trying to optimize this for an experience.”
- **Who it's for, in order (Tyler):**
  1. People who already know his name (friends, peers, people he has met or
     applied to), checking who he is.
  2. People who found one of his projects and want to see who made it.
  3. Possible clients, who are handed quietly to Cirlet.
- **How they arrive (evidence):** almost all arrive by direct link. Search
  brings almost nobody: 6 clicks in 3 months, nearly all for “tyler mayberry”.
  Most real visitors are in the US, and about a third use phones, mainly
  iPhone Safari.
- **What they should leave with:** “Tyler makes real things, lots of them,
  with care, and I'd like to talk to him.”
- **The feeling:** a quiet working room. Calm, warm, discovered rather than
  presented.

## Voice

- The voice is not meant to be strong; the work does the showing. First
  person, plain, warm and understated. Keep the accepted v49 method: plain
  English first, then Tyler's rhythm.
- Functional labels are short. Headings use sentence case.
- Proofread everything. One careless detail undercuts a claim of care.
- Live example: “When I want to follow an idea and work on it, this is the
  place: my personal working studio, projects and the things around them
  sharing the room.”

## Truth and privacy

- Prototypes show building and judgment. They are not traction; never invent
  users, revenue or endorsements.
- Education: “6 years studying at Oklahoma State University in Stillwater,
  Oklahoma”, with no degree details.
- Private vision scripts are aspirations, never biography.
- New personal details, photos or private research need Tyler's approval
  before publishing.
- Keep the About and Press content that preserves search unless Tyler asks.
